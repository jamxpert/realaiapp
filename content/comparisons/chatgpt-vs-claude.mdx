## Quick verdict

**Choose ChatGPT** if you want one app that does everything: chat, images, voice, web search and files, with the cheapest paid plan. **Choose Claude** if most of your work is writing or reading long documents, and you want text that sounds natural.

## Round 1: Free plan

ChatGPT's free plan gives daily chat with limits on uploads, images and deep research. It also includes image making, voice chat and web search. Claude's free plan gives daily chat and file uploads, with lower message limits that reset every few hours. Check each website for the current limits.

ChatGPT's free plan includes more features, while Claude's free plan is focused on chat and documents.

**Winner:** ChatGPT, for more features on the free plan

## Round 2: Price

| Plan | ChatGPT | Claude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheapest paid plan | Go — $8/month | Pro — $20/month ($17/month billed yearly) |
| Other plans | Plus — $20/month | — |

On price alone, ChatGPT has the cheaper paid plan. These are US prices; app store prices in Pakistan may be different.

**Winner:** ChatGPT, on price

## Round 3: Writing quality

Both write well. Claude is widely known for natural, less repetitive writing that needs less editing, and it handles long documents, such as reports, contracts and book chapters, very well. ChatGPT is quick and flexible, and is great for short pieces, ideas and changing the tone of a text.

A simple test you can try yourself: give both the same three tasks (a polite email, a blog intro and a CV summary) and keep the one whose answers you edit least.

**Winner:** Claude, for long and natural-sounding writing

## Round 4: Urdu and Roman Urdu

Both tools understand and reply in Urdu and Roman Urdu. Quality varies by task: everyday chat works well in both, while formal Urdu writing can need more corrections. We're still testing both side by side and will update this round with examples.

**Winner:** Tie for now

## Round 5: Everyday tasks

For quick questions, planning, summaries and small tasks, ChatGPT has the edge because it does more in one place: it can search the web, make images, talk by voice and remember your preferences. Claude is just as good at summaries and planning, but has fewer extra features.

**Winner:** ChatGPT, for doing more in one app

## Final verdict: it depends

ChatGPT wins on price, free features and everyday convenience. Claude wins on writing quality and long documents. If you're not sure, start with ChatGPT's free plan, and try Claude's free plan the next time you have a long piece to write. Many people end up using both.
