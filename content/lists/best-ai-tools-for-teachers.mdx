Teachers spend hours on planning, worksheets and slides. These tools cut that time down, and all of them have free plans.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt","gemini","gamma","notebooklm","napkin-ai","canva"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for lesson plans, quizzes and worksheets

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Ask for a lesson plan for a topic and class level, 10 quiz questions with answers, or an explanation written for a 12-year-old.

## 2. Gemini: for teachers who use Google Classroom and Docs

<ToolCard tool="gemini" />

Gemini works with Google Docs, Slides and Gmail, so it fits well if your school uses Google.

## 3. Gamma: for lesson slides

<ToolCard tool="gamma" />

Turn a topic or your notes into a clean set of slides in minutes, then edit them for your class.

## 4. NotebookLM: for working from your own material

<ToolCard tool="notebooklm" />

Upload your textbook chapters or notes and NotebookLM answers questions only from them, makes study guides and even an audio summary.

## 5. Napkin AI: for diagrams and visuals

<ToolCard tool="napkin-ai" />

Paste a paragraph and Napkin turns it into a diagram or infographic you can use on slides and worksheets.

## 6. Canva: for posters and classroom materials

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Canva has free templates for worksheets, posters and certificates. Teachers can apply for Canva for Education for free.

<Callout type="warning">
Check AI-made questions and answers before using them in class. AI tools sometimes give wrong answers confidently.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Start with ChatGPT or Gemini** for planning.
- **Add Gamma and Napkin AI** to make slides and visuals faster.
- **Use NotebookLM** when you want answers only from your own material.
