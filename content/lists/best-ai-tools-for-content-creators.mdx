Making content means ideas, scripts, editing, thumbnails and posting, again and again. These tools take the slow parts off your hands, so you can post more often.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt","capcut","opus-clip","descript","elevenlabs","canva"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for ideas, titles and scripts

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Ask for 20 video ideas in your niche, a hook for the first 5 seconds or a full script. Then rewrite it in your own voice.

## 2. CapCut: for editing Reels, Shorts and TikToks

<ToolCard tool="capcut" />

CapCut has auto-captions, templates, effects and background removal, on your phone or computer. Most creators can do everything on the free plan.

## 3. OpusClip: for turning long videos into Shorts

<ToolCard tool="opus-clip" />

Upload a long YouTube video or podcast and OpusClip finds the best moments and cuts them into vertical clips with captions.

## 4. Descript: for editing talking videos like a document

<ToolCard tool="descript" />

Descript writes out your video as text. Delete a sentence from the text and it's cut from the video too. Great for podcasts and talking-head videos.

## 5. ElevenLabs: for natural voice-overs

<ToolCard tool="elevenlabs" />

If you don't want to record your own voice, ElevenLabs makes the most natural AI voice-overs. It can also dub your videos into other languages.

## 6. Canva: for thumbnails and posts

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Canva has thousands of YouTube thumbnail and Instagram templates, plus AI tools to remove backgrounds and generate images.

<Callout type="warning">
Check each platform's rules on AI content. YouTube and others ask you to label realistic AI-generated video.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Short-form creators:** CapCut and Canva are enough to start, both free.
- **Long-form YouTubers:** add OpusClip to turn every video into several Shorts.
- **Faceless channels:** ChatGPT for scripts and ElevenLabs for the voice.
