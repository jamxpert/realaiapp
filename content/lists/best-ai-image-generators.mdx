Each AI image generator has a strength: art quality, readable text, free images or safe commercial use. Pick the one that matches what you need the image for.

## Quick comparison

## 1. Midjourney: the most beautiful, artistic images

<ToolCard tool="midjourney" />

Midjourney makes the most striking images, great for art, concepts and moodboards. There's no free plan.

## 2. Ideogram: for posters and images with text

<ToolCard tool="ideogram" />

Most AI tools mess up words in images. Ideogram gets text right, so it's the best for posters, logos and social posts.

## 3. Leonardo.Ai: for lots of free images every day

<ToolCard tool="leonardo-ai" />

Leonardo gives you free credits every day and many styles to choose from, so it's good for trying ideas without paying.

## 4. Adobe Firefly: for commercial work that's safe to use

<ToolCard tool="adobe-firefly" />

Firefly is trained on licensed images, so it's the safest choice for client work. It's also built into Photoshop.

## 5. ChatGPT: for easy images by just chatting

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

ChatGPT can make and edit images in a normal conversation. Just describe what you want and ask for changes.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Art and concepts:** Midjourney.
- **Anything with words in it:** Ideogram.
- **Free daily images:** Leonardo.Ai or ChatGPT.
- **Client and commercial work:** Adobe Firefly.
