As a freelancer, you are the writer, designer, salesperson and accountant. These tools help with the work around the work, so you have more time for paid projects.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt","grammarly","canva","fireflies-ai","notion","gamma"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for proposals and client messages

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Use it to write a proposal for a job post, reply to a difficult client politely or turn a client's messy brief into a clear task list.

## 2. Grammarly: for clear, professional English

<ToolCard tool="grammarly" />

If English isn't your first language, Grammarly helps your proposals and messages sound professional. Clients notice.

## 3. Canva: for portfolios and quick designs

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Make a portfolio, a profile banner, invoices or social posts in minutes, even if you're not a designer.

## 4. Fireflies.ai: for client call notes

<ToolCard tool="fireflies-ai" />

Fireflies records your client calls and writes notes and action items, so you never forget what the client asked for.

## 5. Notion: for keeping projects organised

<ToolCard tool="notion" />

Track clients, deadlines and notes in one place. Notion's free plan is enough for most freelancers.

## 6. Gamma: for pitch decks and reports

<ToolCard tool="gamma" />

Make a pitch deck or a project report for a client in minutes.

<Callout type="warning">
Don't paste a client's private data into AI tools without their permission, and check whether your client allows AI-made work.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Start with ChatGPT and Grammarly.** Better proposals win more work.
- **Add Canva** for your portfolio and profile.
- **Add Fireflies.ai and Notion** once you have several clients at once.
