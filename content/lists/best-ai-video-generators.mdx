There are two kinds of AI video generators. Some create realistic clips from a text prompt or a photo. Others make a talking presenter read your script. This list covers the best of both.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["kling-ai","runway","heygen","synthesia"]} />

## 1. Kling AI: best value for realistic clips

<ToolCard tool="kling-ai" />

Kling makes realistic video clips from a text prompt or a photo, with good movement, at a low price. A good first tool to try.

## 2. Runway: for filmmakers who want more control

<ToolCard tool="runway" />

Runway gives you more control over camera moves, styles and editing. It's popular with filmmakers and creative agencies.

## 3. HeyGen: for talking avatars and video translation

<ToolCard tool="heygen" />

HeyGen makes videos where an AI presenter, or a copy of you, reads your script. It can also translate your own videos with lip sync.

## 4. Synthesia: for company training videos

<ToolCard tool="synthesia" />

Synthesia is built for businesses: training, onboarding and how-to videos with AI presenters in many languages.

<Callout type="warning">
Never make a video of a real person without their permission. Platforms remove deepfakes, and in many countries it's illegal.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Short creative clips:** start with Kling AI, then try Runway if you need more control.
- **A presenter reading a script:** HeyGen for marketing and social media, Synthesia for training.
