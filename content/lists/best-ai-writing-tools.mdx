Some AI tools write a full draft for you. Others fix and improve what you've already written. Most writers use one of each.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["claude","chatgpt","grammarly","quillbot"]} />

## 1. Claude: for long, natural-sounding writing

<ToolCard tool="claude" />

Claude writes in a natural, human tone and handles long documents well. It's a favourite for articles, reports and editing long drafts.

## 2. ChatGPT: for all-round writing help

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

ChatGPT is great for first drafts, ideas, outlines and changing the tone of a text. It also searches the web when you need facts.

## 3. Grammarly: for fixing grammar everywhere you type

<ToolCard tool="grammarly" />

Grammarly checks spelling, grammar and tone in your browser, email, Word and phone keyboard.

## 4. QuillBot: for rewriting and summarising

<ToolCard tool="quillbot" />

QuillBot rewrites a sentence in different words and summarises long text. Useful when your sentence doesn't sound right.

<Callout type="warning">
AI writing can contain mistakes and made-up facts. Check every fact, and add your own experience so the writing is really yours.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **To write a draft:** Claude or ChatGPT.
- **To fix your own English:** Grammarly.
- **To reword a sentence:** QuillBot.
