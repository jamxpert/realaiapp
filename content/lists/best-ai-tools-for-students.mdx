Every tool on this list has a free plan, so you can try it before you pay anything.

Together, they cover the main parts of student life: understanding topics, researching, writing and presenting.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt", "gemini", "perplexity", "quillbot", "grammarly", "gamma"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for explaining topics and practice questions

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Ask ChatGPT to explain a topic step by step, like a teacher, and then ask follow-up questions until it clicks. It can also make practice questions for your exams. Try: "Explain photosynthesis simply, then give me 5 practice questions and check my answers."

## 2. Gemini: for students who use Google apps

<ToolCard tool="gemini" />

Gemini works with Google Docs, Gmail and Drive, so it fits well if your school uses Google. It's similar to ChatGPT for studying, and its paid plan is one of the cheapest.

## 3. Perplexity: for research with sources

<ToolCard tool="perplexity" />

Perplexity answers questions with links to its sources, so you can check facts and find references for assignments. Always open the sources and read them yourself.

## 4. QuillBot: for rewriting your own sentences

<ToolCard tool="quillbot" />

QuillBot helps when your own sentence doesn't sound right, and its summariser shortens long readings. Use it on your own writing, not to disguise someone else's.

<Callout type="warning">
Check your school or university rules before using AI for assignments. Paraphrasing someone else's work is still plagiarism.
</Callout>

## 5. Grammarly: for checking grammar and spelling

<ToolCard tool="grammarly" />

Grammarly checks spelling and grammar as you type in your browser, Word and email. The free plan is enough to catch most mistakes in essays and applications.

## 6. Gamma: for quick presentations

<ToolCard tool="gamma" />

Paste your notes and Gamma makes a designed presentation in minutes. Edit the text and check every fact before you present. Read our [Gamma review](/reviews/gamma-review).

## Which one should you choose?

- **Start with ChatGPT** for studying, and **Grammarly** for your writing.
- **Add Perplexity** when you need sources for an assignment.
- **Use Gamma** when a presentation is due.
