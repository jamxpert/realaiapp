Automation tools connect your apps so work happens by itself: a new form entry goes to a sheet, an email gets summarised, a message gets sent. All three tools here can add AI steps too.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["zapier","make","n8n"]} />

## 1. Zapier: the easiest to use

<ToolCard tool="zapier" />

Zapier connects thousands of apps, and you can describe an automation in plain words and let it build it. The best place to start.

## 2. Make: for bigger automations at a lower price

<ToolCard tool="make" />

Make shows your automation as a visual map, handles complex logic and costs less than Zapier for large volumes.

## 3. n8n: for technical users, free if self-hosted

<ToolCard tool="n8n" />

n8n is very powerful, great for AI agents, and free if you run it on your own server.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Beginners:** Zapier.
- **Lots of automations on a budget:** Make.
- **Developers or technical teams:** n8n.
