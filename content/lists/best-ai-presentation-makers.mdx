## 1. Gamma: best overall

<ToolCard tool="gamma" />

Type a topic or paste your notes and Gamma makes a full, designed presentation. You can export it to PowerPoint or PDF.

## 2. Beautiful.ai: for business presentations

<ToolCard tool="beautiful-ai" />

Beautiful.ai keeps your slides neat as you edit, so they always look professional. Good for company pitches and reports.

## 3. Napkin AI: for diagrams and infographics

<ToolCard tool="napkin-ai" />

Napkin turns a paragraph into a clear diagram you can add to any presentation.

## 4. Canva: for full design control

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Canva has thousands of presentation templates, plus AI to write and design slides, if you want to customise every detail.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Fastest full presentation:** Gamma.
- **Polished business slides:** Beautiful.ai.
- **Add diagrams to your slides:** Napkin AI.
