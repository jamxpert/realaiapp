All five chatbots here are free to start. They're all good at everyday questions, but each has something it does best.

## Quick comparison

## 1. ChatGPT: best all-rounder

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

ChatGPT does almost everything: writing, images, web search, voice chat and file analysis. The easiest choice if you only want one.

## 2. Claude: best for writing and long documents

<ToolCard tool="claude" />

Claude writes more naturally and is excellent with long PDFs and careful reasoning.

## 3. Gemini: best if you use Google

<ToolCard tool="gemini" />

Gemini works with Gmail, Docs, Drive and Android, and has a cheap paid plan.

## 4. Microsoft Copilot: best free option for Microsoft users

<ToolCard tool="microsoft-copilot" />

Copilot is free to chat with and works inside Word, Excel and PowerPoint with Microsoft 365.

## 5. DeepSeek: free and strong at maths and coding

<ToolCard tool="deepseek" />

DeepSeek is completely free to chat with and is very good at logic, maths and code.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Only want one:** ChatGPT.
- **Lots of writing or long documents:** Claude.
- **You live in Google or Microsoft apps:** Gemini or Copilot.
