Most office work is writing, reading, meetings and slides. These six tools cover all four, and most of them have a free plan you can start with today.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt","microsoft-copilot","claude","otter-ai","gamma","notion"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for everyday writing and thinking

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Use it to draft emails, rewrite a message so it sounds polite, explain a report you don't understand or plan your week. If you only use one AI tool at work, start here.

## 2. Microsoft Copilot: if your office runs on Microsoft 365

<ToolCard tool="microsoft-copilot" />

Copilot works inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook, so you can summarise a long email thread or turn a Word document into slides without copying anything.

## 3. Claude: for long documents and careful writing

<ToolCard tool="claude" />

Claude is very good at reading long PDFs, contracts and reports and answering questions about them. Its writing also sounds more natural than most.

## 4. Otter.ai: for meeting notes

<ToolCard tool="otter-ai" />

Otter joins your Zoom, Google Meet or Teams calls, writes down what everyone says and sends a summary with action items afterwards.

## 5. Gamma: for presentations in minutes

<ToolCard tool="gamma" />

Paste your notes or a topic and Gamma makes a clean, designed presentation. You still need to check and edit it, but the first draft takes minutes, not hours.

## 6. Notion: for notes, tasks and documents in one place

<ToolCard tool="notion" />

Notion keeps your notes, to-do lists and team documents together, and its AI can search across them and write summaries.

<Callout type="warning">
Don't paste confidential company data, client details or passwords into a free AI tool. Check your company's AI policy first.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Start with ChatGPT or Claude** for writing. Try both free plans for a week and keep the one you like.
- **Use Microsoft Copilot** if your company already pays for Microsoft 365. Ask your IT team before installing anything else.
- **Add Otter.ai** if you spend more than a few hours a week in meetings.
