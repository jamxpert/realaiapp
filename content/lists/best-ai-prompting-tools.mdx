A good prompt makes a huge difference to what AI gives you. These playgrounds let you test prompts, compare results and change settings that normal chat apps hide.

## 1. Google AI Studio: best free place to test prompts

<ToolCard tool="google-ai-studio" />

Google AI Studio lets you test prompts with Gemini models for free, with full control over settings like temperature.

## 2. Claude Console: for turning an idea into a strong prompt

<ToolCard tool="claude-console" />

Its prompt generator turns a rough idea into a well-structured prompt, and you can test and improve it side by side.

## 3. OpenAI Playground: for apps built on OpenAI

<ToolCard tool="openai-playground" />

Test prompts with OpenAI's models and settings before you put them in your app. You pay per use.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Learning or testing for free:** Google AI Studio.
- **You want help writing the prompt itself:** Claude Console.
- **Your app uses OpenAI:** OpenAI Playground.
