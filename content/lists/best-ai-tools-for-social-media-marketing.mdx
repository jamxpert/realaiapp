Social media needs new content every day. These tools help you write, design and edit posts much faster.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt","canva","capcut","opus-clip","ideogram"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for captions, hooks and content calendars

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Ask for a month of post ideas, 10 caption options or hooks for Reels. Give it your brand's tone and audience.

## 2. Canva: for post and story designs

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Templates for every platform, a brand kit for your colours and fonts, and one-click resizing.

## 3. CapCut: for Reels and TikToks

<ToolCard tool="capcut" />

Templates, trending effects and auto-captions make short videos quick to edit.

## 4. OpusClip: for repurposing long videos

<ToolCard tool="opus-clip" />

Turn one webinar, podcast or YouTube video into many short clips for every platform.

## 5. Ideogram: for ad images with text

<ToolCard tool="ideogram" />

Make eye-catching images with readable text for ads and promotions.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Small team or solo:** ChatGPT, Canva and CapCut cover almost everything, free.
- **You have long videos or webinars:** add OpusClip.
