AI voices now sound close to real people. These tools turn your script into a voice-over for videos, ads, courses or audiobooks, without a microphone.

## 1. ElevenLabs: the most natural voices

<ToolCard tool="elevenlabs" />

ElevenLabs has the most realistic voices, works in many languages and can clone your own voice. It's also the cheapest to start paying for.

## 2. Murf AI: for business and presentation voice-overs

<ToolCard tool="murf-ai" />

Murf makes clean, professional voice-overs and lets you sync the voice with slides or video in the same place.

## 3. Speechify: for listening instead of reading

<ToolCard tool="speechify" />

Speechify reads books, PDFs, articles and emails out loud. It's built for listening, not for making voice-overs to publish.

<Callout type="warning">
Only clone your own voice, or a voice you have written permission to use.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Voice-overs for YouTube, ads or audiobooks:** ElevenLabs.
- **Corporate videos and presentations:** Murf AI.
- **You want to listen to documents:** Speechify.
