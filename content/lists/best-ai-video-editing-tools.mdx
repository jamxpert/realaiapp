AI video editors do the boring parts for you: captions, cutting silences, removing backgrounds and finding the best clips. Here are the four best, each for a different kind of editing.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["capcut","descript","opus-clip","veed"]} />

## 1. CapCut: best overall, and free

<ToolCard tool="capcut" />

CapCut works on phone and computer and has auto-captions, templates, effects and background removal. It's the easiest place to start.

## 2. Descript: for talking videos and podcasts

<ToolCard tool="descript" />

Edit your video by editing its text. Descript also removes filler words like "um" and can fix your voice so it sounds studio-quality.

## 3. OpusClip: for turning long videos into Shorts

<ToolCard tool="opus-clip" />

OpusClip picks the best moments from a long video and makes vertical clips with captions, ready for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

## 4. VEED: for quick edits in the browser

<ToolCard tool="veed" />

VEED runs in your browser with nothing to install. It's good for subtitles, translating captions and quick trims.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Not sure? Start with CapCut.** It's free and covers most needs.
- **You record podcasts or tutorials:** Descript will save you the most time.
- **You have long videos already:** OpusClip turns them into many short clips.
