Stop taking notes by hand. These tools turn your meetings and recordings into text, summaries and action items.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["turboscribe","otter-ai","fireflies-ai"]} />

## 1. TurboScribe: for transcribing recordings cheaply

<ToolCard tool="turboscribe" />

Upload audio or video files and get accurate text in many languages. The free plan and the low-cost unlimited plan are hard to beat.

## 2. Otter.ai: for live notes in meetings

<ToolCard tool="otter-ai" />

Otter joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls, writes live notes and sends a summary afterwards.

## 3. Fireflies.ai: for teams with many calls

<ToolCard tool="fireflies-ai" />

Fireflies records calls, writes notes in many languages and makes every past meeting searchable.

<Callout type="warning">
Tell people before you record a meeting. In many places it's required by law.
</Callout>

## Which one should you choose?

- **Transcribing files** (interviews, lectures, videos): TurboScribe.
- **Live meeting notes:** Otter.ai.
- **A team with lots of calls:** Fireflies.ai.
