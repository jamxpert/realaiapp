You don't need a big team to use AI. These tools help a small business make posts, product photos, ads and customer replies, and most have free plans.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["chatgpt","canva","remove-bg","capcut","zapier"]} />

## 1. ChatGPT: for marketing text and customer replies

<ToolCard tool="chatgpt" />

Write product descriptions, ad text, WhatsApp replies and social posts. Ask it to write in a friendly tone that fits your shop.

## 2. Canva: for posts, flyers and menus

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Canva has templates for social posts, flyers, menus and business cards, with AI to resize designs for every platform.

## 3. remove.bg: for clean product photos

<ToolCard tool="remove-bg" />

Remove the background from product photos in one click, so they look professional on your store or Instagram.

## 4. CapCut: for short product videos

<ToolCard tool="capcut" />

Make Reels and TikToks of your products with templates and auto-captions, straight from your phone.

## 5. Zapier: for automating repeat work

<ToolCard tool="zapier" />

Connect your apps so things happen by themselves, for example, add every new order to a Google Sheet and send yourself a message.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Start with ChatGPT and Canva.** Both are free and cover most marketing.
- **Selling products online:** add remove.bg and CapCut.
- **Spending hours on repeat tasks:** try Zapier.
