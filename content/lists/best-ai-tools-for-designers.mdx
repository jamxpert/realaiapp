AI won't replace a good designer, but it removes hours of repetitive work. These tools help with ideas, images, cut-outs and quick designs.

## Quick comparison

<ComparisonTable tools={["canva","midjourney","adobe-firefly","ideogram","remove-bg","microsoft-designer"]} />

## 1. Canva: for fast designs from templates

<ToolCard tool="canva" />

Canva's AI can write, generate images, remove backgrounds and resize a design for every platform in one click.

## 2. Midjourney: for concepts and moodboards

<ToolCard tool="midjourney" />

Midjourney makes the most artistic images, great for exploring ideas and styles before you start designing.

## 3. Adobe Firefly: for commercial-safe images inside Adobe apps

<ToolCard tool="adobe-firefly" />

Firefly is built into Photoshop and Illustrator and trained on licensed images, so it's safer for client work.

## 4. Ideogram: for designs with readable text

<ToolCard tool="ideogram" />

Ideogram is the best at putting correct text in AI images, so it's useful for posters, logos and ad ideas.

## 5. remove.bg: for background removal

<ToolCard tool="remove-bg" />

One click removes the background from any photo, with clean edges around hair and products.

## 6. Microsoft Designer: for free quick designs

<ToolCard tool="microsoft-designer" />

Microsoft Designer makes free social posts and AI images with just a Microsoft account.

## Which one should you choose?

- **Professional designers:** Adobe Firefly inside the apps you already use, and Midjourney for concepts.
- **Non-designers:** Canva or Microsoft Designer.
- **Text in images:** Ideogram.
