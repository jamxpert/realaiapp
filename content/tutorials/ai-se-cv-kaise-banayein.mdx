Is guide mein aap ChatGPT ki free plan se ek saaf suthra, professional CV banayenge. Aapko sirf apni maloomat aur 20 minute chahiye.

Yeh tareeqa fresh graduates, job badalne walon aur pehli dafa CV banane walon, sab ke liye kaam karta hai.

## Kya chahiye

- Apni taleem, tajurba aur skills ki list
- Ek ChatGPT account (free plan kaafi hai)
- CV design karne ke liye Canva (yeh bhi free plan par chal jata hai)

## Step by step tareeqa

<Step number={1} title="Apni maloomat ek jagah likh lein">
Apna naam, contact, taleem, kaam ka tajurba, skills aur certificates ek note mein likh lein. Jitni saaf maloomat hogi, CV utna behtar banega.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="ChatGPT kholein">
[chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) par jayein aur login karein. Free plan is kaam ke liye kaafi hai. Neeche "Ask anything" wale box mein aap prompt paste karenge.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Yeh prompt copy karke paste karein">
Neeche wala prompt copy karein. Brackets wali jagah apni maloomat likhein.

<PromptBox>
You are a professional CV writer. Create a one-page, ATS-friendly CV in English for a [JOB TITLE] role, using only the details below. Do not invent any experience, degrees or skills. Use short bullet points that start with action verbs.

My details:
[PASTE YOUR DETAILS HERE]
</PromptBox>
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Natija dhyan se check karein">
AI kabhi kabhi ghalat ya bana hua kaam likh deta hai. Har line parhein aur jo cheez sach nahi, use hata dein.

<Callout type="warning">
Apna CNIC number, bank details ya ghar ka poora address AI ko na dein. CV ke liye in ki zaroorat nahi.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step number={5} title="Canva mein design karein">
[canva.com](https://www.canva.com) par search box mein "Resume" likhein aur ek saada, professional template chunein. Phir ChatGPT wala text us mein paste kar dein.

Simple template behtar hai: ek ya do rang, saaf font aur koi bhaari design nahi. Kai companies CV ko software (ATS) se parhti hain, aur bohat zyada design wala CV software theek se nahi parh pata.
</Step>

<Step number={6} title="PDF mein save karein">
CV ko PDF mein download karein, taa ke har computer par wohi design nazar aaye.
</Step>

<Callout type="pakistan">
Job portals par apply karte waqt CV hamesha PDF mein upload karein. File ka naam saaf rakhein, jaise "Ali-Khan-CV.pdf", aur har job ke liye job ad ke keywords CV mein shamil karein. ChatGPT se kahein: "Is job ad ke mutabiq mera CV update karo", aur job ad paste kar dein.
</Callout>
