## What is Gamma?

Gamma makes presentations, documents and web pages with AI. You describe what you need, and it creates a first draft that you can edit.

You can start from a short topic, such as "a 10-slide pitch for a home bakery", or paste your own notes or a document so the slides use your content. Gamma first shows you an outline of slide titles. You can change it, then pick a theme, and it builds the full deck with layouts and images.

## What we like

- **Speed.** Going from an idea to a complete, designed deck takes minutes instead of hours.
- **The outline step.** You approve the structure before any slides are made, so you stay in control of the content.
- **Using your own notes.** Pasting your notes gives far more accurate slides than a one-line topic.
- **Easy export.** You can present from Gamma, or export to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides.

## What could be better

- **Free credits don't refill.** Once you use the 400 sign-up credits, you need to pay to keep generating.
- **Generic text.** AI-written slides can sound general. Add your own examples and numbers.
- **Facts need checking.** Like every AI tool, Gamma can get facts wrong. Check every number before you present.
- **Export fixes.** Some layouts shift slightly in PowerPoint, so check each slide after exporting.

## Should you use Gamma?

If you make presentations now and then, for class, a client pitch or a team meeting, Gamma is worth trying on the free plan. It saves the most time on the first draft. If you make decks every week, Plus is fairly priced. If you need slides that follow a strict company template, a tool like Beautiful.ai or plain PowerPoint may suit you better.
